Aluminium Extrusions

Inserts 20 mm slot series aluminium extrusion profiles as new components at the length you enter.

How to use

  1. Design workspace, Solid tab, Create panel: click Aluminum Extrusion (next to Pipe).
  2. Choose a profile: 2020, 2040, 2060, 2080, 4040 or C4080.
  3. Enter the length in millimetres and click OK.
  4. Position the new component with Move/Copy or joints. The profile is centred on the component origin.
  5. To change a length later, edit the extrude feature inside the timeline group.

Notes

  • Profiles are nominal geometry. Check them against your supplier drawing for tight-fit work.
  • Each insert is a normal Fusion component with a sketch and an extrude feature.
